# 05 — Extensions Table

Extensions are short tags attached directly to a character name, in
parentheses, with no space before the opening parenthesis in most house
styles (`JANE (V.O.)`).

| Extension | Meaning | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| `(V.O.)` | Voice Over | Narration, phone voice, internal monologue heard by the audience but not other characters on screen |
| `(O.S.)` / `(O.C.)` | Off Screen / Off Camera | Character is present in the scene's space but not currently in frame |
| `(CONT'D)` | Continued | Same character's dialogue resumes after an interruption (an action line, or a page break) |

Dual dialogue (two characters speaking simultaneously, typically laid out
side-by-side in two columns) is a distinct structural feature, not an
extension — see `08-archetypes.md` and `examples/dual-dialogue.fountain`.

## Why this rule exists

`(V.O.)` and `(O.S.)` exist because sound and picture aren't always
describing the same thing at the same moment — the page needs a compact
way to say "you'll hear this character but not see them," which matters
enormously for sound and picture department scheduling. `(CONT'D)` exists
purely to prevent confusion when a page break or a brief action
interruption might make a reader think a new speech has started.
